# Pricing Strategy Kickoff

Sarah opened the meeting by reviewing current tiered pricing ($29/$79/$249 monthly).
Marcus raised concern that the $79 tier is underperforming — only 8% of signups
convert to it, most either stay on $29 or jump straight to $249.

Priya proposed testing a usage-based add-on instead of a fixed middle tier, billed
per API call above a base allowance.

## Decisions
- Freeze current pricing for Q1, no changes before March.
- Priya to draft a usage-based pricing proposal for review by Feb 1.
