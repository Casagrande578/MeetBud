# Pricing Follow-up — Usage-Based Model Review

Priya presented the usage-based pricing proposal from January. Diego (finance) flagged
that unpredictable billing could hurt enterprise adoption — large customers prefer
flat, predictable invoices.

After discussion, the group decided against a pure usage-based model. Instead they'll
keep the three-tier structure but reduce the middle tier price from $79 to $59 and
add higher API limits to make it more attractive.

## Decisions
- Reject usage-based billing for now — enterprise customers dislike unpredictable costs.
- Reduce middle tier from $79/mo to $59/mo, effective April 1.
- Increase middle tier API call limit from 10k to 25k/month.
